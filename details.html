Deep Dive Scams & Social Engineering

Phishing & Scams

Phishing is a technique used to trick people into revealing passwords, card numbers, or personal information. It usually arrives as a message that looks familiar — an email, a text, or a social media DM.

A Real-World Scenario

You receive an email that looks like it is from your university saying your account will be locked in 24 hours unless you "verify" your password. The link goes to a page that looks almost identical to the real login page. If you enter your password there, the attacker captures it.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Urgent language: "act now", "account will be closed".
  • Sender address that does not match the organisation.
  • Links that point to a different domain when you hover over them.
  • Requests for passwords, codes, or payment details.
  • Unexpected attachments.

Phishing vs Legitimate Email

FeaturePhishing SignLegitimate Sign
Sender addressSlight misspelling or unknown domainMatches official domain
ToneUrgent, threateningCalm, informational
LinksHover shows unrelated URLHover shows official site
RequestsAsks for password or codeNever asks for credentials
