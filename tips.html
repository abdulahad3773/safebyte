Digital Safety Tips & Quick Reference

Simple actions you can use to stay safer online.

01

Phishing Recognition

  • Hover over links before clicking.
  • Verify the sender's full domain.
  • Never enter passwords from email links.
  • Contact organisations directly when unsure.
02

Password Management

  • Use a unique password for every account.
  • Length matters more than complexity alone.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
  • Use a dedicated password manager.
03

Privacy Settings

  • Restrict who can view your posts.
  • Audit mobile app location permissions.
  • Avoid broadcasting live locations.
  • Review connected third-party apps.
04

Cyberbullying Response

  • Do not engage or reply to abusive messages.
  • Capture screenshots with timestamps.
  • Use built-in block and report tools.
  • Reach out to a trusted adult or counsellor.
05

Misinformation Checking

  • Trace claims back to the primary source.
  • Check publication dates for recycled news.
  • Cross-check across independent outlets.
  • Be sceptical of sensational headlines.

Privacy Quick-Check

  • Use a different password for each important account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication when it is available.
  • Review who can see your social media posts.
  • Check which apps can use your location.
  • Install updates on your devices.
