Frequently Asked Questions

Digital Safety FAQ

Click on any question below to reveal practical steps for staying safe online.

How can I recognize a phishing message before clicking?
  • Hover over links to preview the actual destination URL before clicking.
  • Verify the sender's full email domain, not just their display name.
  • Never enter passwords or banking details from links inside emails or texts.
  • Contact the organisation directly using their official website when unsure.
What is the best way to manage all my passwords?
  • Use a unique password for every single account.
  • Focus on length (12+ characters) rather than complex symbols alone.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever available.
  • Use a dedicated password manager so you only have to remember one master password.
Which privacy settings should I check on my devices and social media?
  • Restrict who can view your posts and personal profile details.
  • Audit mobile app location permissions and set them to "While Using" or "Never."
  • Avoid broadcasting your live location in real time.
  • Regularly review and revoke access for connected third-party apps.
How should I respond if I experience or witness cyberbullying?
  • Do not engage or reply to abusive messages.
  • Capture screenshots with visible timestamps and usernames as evidence.
  • Use the platform's built-in block and report tools.
  • Reach out to a trusted adult, teacher, or counsellor for support.
How can I tell if an online news story or claim is misinformation?
  • Trace claims back to the primary source or original study.
  • Check publication dates to ensure old news isn't being recycled out of context.
  • Cross-check the story across multiple independent, reputable outlets.
  • Be sceptical of sensational, emotionally charged headlines designed to provoke outrage.
Are the tools on SafeByte safe to test my real passwords on?

No—never enter your real passwords into any online checker. Our password tool runs locally in your browser and never sends data anywhere, but you should always use sample passwords of a similar length and style when testing.

Privacy Quick-Check

  • Use a different password for each important account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication when it is available.
  • Review who can see your social media posts.
  • Check which apps can use your location.
  • Install updates on your devices.
